## Problems and routines

| Problem | Routine | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compatible-pair smooth coefficient objective, including ridge and differentiable lq penalties | `scipy.optimize.minimize`, default BFGS | Smooth unconstrained objective with analytic gradient. |
| Arbitrary-link GRR, including weighted population fits | `scipy.optimize.minimize`, default BFGS | Same smooth, potentially nonconvex objective and analytic gradient; no Hessian modification. |
| Exact l1 penalty, including weighted AutoDML l1 objectives | `scipy.optimize.minimize`, default L-BFGS-B | Write each coefficient as the difference of two nonnegative variables. The penalty becomes linear in their sum. |
| l1 coefficient ball, with no penalty, l1 penalty, or ridge | `scipy.optimize.minimize`, default SLSQP | The same nonnegative parts turn the ball into a linear constraint. Ridge remains in the original smooth objective. |
| Weighted compatible-pair population objective | `scipy.optimize.minimize`, default BFGS | Direct analytic weighted objective and gradient, without a separate Newton implementation. |
| Entropy-balancing dual | `scipy.optimize.minimize`, default BFGS | Original `sum(exp(Phi c)) - c' target`, original zero initial vector, analytic gradient. The objective is not rescaled. |
| AutoDML penalty-loading updates | `scipy.optimize.fixed_point(method="iteration")` | Executes the same unaccelerated loading update. The method argument preserves that update; iteration limits and convergence tolerance are library defaults. Each update solves the original fixed-loading objective. |
| Legacy numerical GRR, outcome GLM, and legacy density-ratio fits | Existing SciPy L-BFGS-B | Keep their existing objectives and statistical routes, while removing numerical setting overrides. |
| Weight-program SciPy backend | Existing SciPy SLSQP | Existing constraints and objective are preserved; numerical settings use library defaults. |
| Scalar population-design root in E-14 and lq proximal roots | `scipy.optimize.brentq` | Scalar bracketed roots use the standard routine and its numerical defaults. The proximal helper is no longer used by coefficient fitting. |
| Logistic propensity and logistic cross-validation | Existing scikit-learn `LogisticRegression` and `LogisticRegressionCV` | Preserve penalties, predictors and CV grids; omit numerical solver overrides. |

For l1, `beta = positive - negative`, both parts are nonnegative, and the penalty is `lambda * sum(positive + negative)`. Minimizing over the parts recovers exactly `lambda * ||beta||_1`. A constraint on their sum at most the radius is equivalent, after minimizing over the parts, to the original coefficient ball. Weighted l1 penalties use the original coordinate weights. Offsets remain fixed; both penalties and balls continue to apply to deviations from the offset.

Compatible-pair trial points outside any required dual or representer domain have objective `+inf`; their unavailable gradients are NaN. Required fitting and additional evaluation rows are preserved. No clipping, line-search retry, unrestricted preliminary solve, support polishing, alternative optimizer, or custom acceptance threshold is used to rescue a fit. Library failures and invalid returned solutions cannot predict. SciPy messages are preserved, including precision-loss failures. Iteration exhaustion maps to `maxit`; other optimizer failures map to `optimizer_failure`, now included in the statuses API. Non-finite solutions and infeasible starts retain their failure statuses.

`newton_solve`, `fista_solve`, `coordinate_descent`, and `eb_dual_newton` remain callable names, but their optimization is performed by the routines above. `prox_residual` is NaN and `polished` is false for SciPy coefficient fits. The split problem's projected-gradient residual and, where available from SciPy, the ball multiplier are diagnostics, never success gates. No new tolerance is introduced to infer a support from nearly zero coefficients. The reported objective evaluates the original coefficient penalty.

## Removed numerical settings and optional parameters

- Damped Newton: 500 iterations, 60 halvings, `tol=1e-10`, target-dependent tolerance scaling, Armijo `1e-4`, Hessian singularity ratio `1e-13`, and fraction-to-boundary shrink `0.01`.
- Arbitrary-link Newton: the same iteration and line-search controls, the `1e-8` Hessian floor, and the positive-Hessian convergence threshold `1e-12`.
- FISTA: 100000 iterations, 60 backtracking attempts, `tol=1e-6*lambda` or `1e-8`, Lipschitz decrease `0.9`, Newton polishing toward `1e-12`, and its 50-iteration and 60-halving budgets.
- EB Newton: 200 iterations, scaled `tol=1e-8`, Armijo `1e-4`, rounding-based acceptance and the `1e-12` line-search cutoff.
- Population Newton: 500 iterations, 60 halvings, `tol=1e-13`, Armijo `1e-4`, and a custom Hessian singularity threshold.
- AutoDML: 10000 inner sweeps, coefficient-change tolerance `1e-10`, 10 outer updates, and absolute outer tolerance `1e-8`.
- Legacy L-BFGS-B and outcome fitting: `maxiter=500` and `ftol=1e-8` overrides.
- Logistic regression: `max_iter=10000`, `tol=1e-10`; logistic CV: `max_iter=10000`, `tol=1e-8`.
- Weight-program SLSQP: `maxiter=1000`, `ftol=1e-15`.
- E-14 Brent root: `xtol=1e-15`; lq proximal bisection: 200 hand-written updates.
- Boundary counting defaults of `1e-8` in fitting entry points are replaced by optional `boundary_tol=None`. An explicit boundary tolerance only requests a diagnostic count. The l1 activity diagnostic's `active_rtol` is also optional, default None.

Coefficient routines expose `max_iter=None`, `tol=None`, and `options=None`. `None` passes no numerical override to the library. GRRGLM, outcome fitting, general-link fitting, density-ratio estimation, model selection and estimation propagate optional iteration and tolerance settings without inserting values. Direct coefficient routines, general-link fitting, population fitting and EB accept SciPy option dictionaries. The weight-program `solver_options=None` now also applies to its SciPy backend. Root helpers accept `options=None` for library keyword arguments. Logistic helpers expose optional `max_iter` and `tol` and omit them from estimator construction when None. AutoDML outer fits expose `max_iter=None`, `tol=None`, and `inner_options=None`; their outer `tol` maps to SciPy `fixed_point`'s `xtol` only when explicitly provided.

E-12 through E-24 fitting calls no longer pass the removed numerical overrides. There are no separate E-16, E-17, or E-23 fitting modules in `grrexp`; the experiment notebooks were inspected for embedded optimizer overrides and none were found. E-12 and E-13 balance summaries and E-20 gradient summaries retain values as diagnostics rather than rejecting library-successful fits at the old gradient threshold. E-21 EB weight differences are also diagnostics, not another convergence rule. Missing fit records and the algebraic estimator identities still fail their checks. The separate population verification conditions, quadrature checks, and statistical hypotheses have not been weakened to make the new optimizer pass.

RieszNet's cited training schedule and settings are unchanged. Model specifications, regularization levels, dictionary restrictions, CV grids, seeds, sample sizes and statistical evaluation rules are unchanged. Gradient boosting's tuned number of boosting stages is an estimator hyperparameter and remains in its grid; it is not a numerical convergence tolerance. Built-in generators used by these experiments have analytic inverse links, so the generic user-defined generator's numerical inverse is not a traversed experiment solver and was not changed. NumPy direct linear algebra and non-iterative projections are retained.

### 母集団の最小化

`scipy.optimize.minimize(method="trust-ncg", jac=analytic_gradient, hess=analytic_hessian)` を使用する。これは **chosen by the implementer for Claude's review**。理由は、これらの目的関数の解析的 Hessian をそのまま利用でき、BKL・BP の定義域外の試行点を目的関数 `+inf` として棄却できるため。許容値、反復上限、trust region の設定は実装者が指定せず、SciPy の既定値を用いる。利用者が明示的に指定するための既存の任意パラメータは残した。

最初に検討した `trust-exact` は、境界付近の BKL のテストで定義域外の試行点の Hessian を評価し、SciPy の `asarray_chkfinite` が `ValueError: array must not contain infs or NaNs` を発生させた。このため実装方法を `trust-ncg` に変更した。実行時に別の方法へ切り替える処理ではない。独自の Hessian 修正、再試行、代替計算は追加していない。

- `grrexp.population.solve`: 共通の重み付き母集団目的関数と解析的 Hessian。求積重みの和を正規化せず、その和、基準 1、旧許容幅 `1e-14` と一致判定を `PopulationSolution.diagnostics` に記録する。形状と負の重みの検査は維持する。
- `GRRGeneralLink.fit`: 任意の `method` 引数を追加し、`trust-ncg` では既存の解析的 Hessian を渡す。既定の標本当てはめは BFGS のまま。E-13 の `_general_fit` と multistart の母集団計算だけ `trust-ncg` を明示する。
- E-12 `population_cell`・`stage0`、E-13 `population_compatible`・`population_incompatible`・`stage0`、E-15 `population_fit`・`stage0`、E-20 `population_ratio`・`stage0`、E-17 notebook の母集団計算から、状態・メッセージ・求積診断を出力へ渡す。
